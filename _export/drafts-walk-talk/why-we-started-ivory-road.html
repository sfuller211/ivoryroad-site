When people ask us why we started Ivory Road, the answer always comes back to this: we love working together, and we love encouraging others.

For us, Ivory Road isn't just a shop or a brand, it's an overflow of who we are and what we believe. Over the years, God has given us experiences, lessons, and even creative quirks that we don't want to keep to ourselves. Whether it's designing Ruby Doodle coloring books, writing devotionals, or turning ordinary things into something beautiful in our gardens and floral arrangements, real or faux, we've learned that creativity can become a gift to others.

At its heart, Ivory Road is meant to be a place our community can trust. Trust that what we share will be rooted in our faith. Trust that our products and words are born out of generosity, not just business. Trust that when you stop by, you'll find encouragement for your own journey.

Yes, we'll have products for sale, but sales are not the end goal. Our mission is always bigger: to use what we produce to make a difference. Sometimes that looks like offering a free devotional to someone who needs encouragement. Other times it may mean donating a floral arrangement to lift the spirit of someone in need, using our products to raise funds for worthy causes, or giving away resources that point people back to God's comfort and hope.

We believe in giving out of abundance, and that when we do, God multiplies it. Ivory Road is our way of opening the doors wide, sharing what we've been given, and reminding others that beauty, faith, and encouragement can meet us right where we are.

So welcome, friend. We're glad you're here on this journey with us.
