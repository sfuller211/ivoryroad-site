Two years ago, Ruby Doodle started with nothing more than a big dream. What began as simple coloring pages has now grown into a community of 17,000 friends who share in the joy of creativity and encouragement.

Over the past two years, Ruby Doodle has filled homes and hearts with color. It has encouraged families, sparked imagination, and reminded us all that a few minutes with a coloring page can feel like a deep breath in the middle of a busy day. What began as a simple idea has turned into a way to share encouragement and creativity with people all around the world, and that's because of you.

So first, thank you. Thank you for every page you've colored, every message you've sent, and every time you've shared Ruby Doodle with a friend. You've helped us build something that's bigger than books. You've helped us create a space where creativity and encouragement meet.

And now, as Ruby Doodle celebrates its second birthday, we're excited to share the next part of the journey with you. Ruby Doodle is joining with all of our creative projects, from devotionals and books to florals and gifts, under a new home called Ivory Road.

Ivory Road is where Ruby Doodle will continue to grow, alongside new resources designed to encourage, inspire, and uplift. It's a place rooted in our faith and overflowing with creativity, where we'll keep offering coloring books you love, while also opening the door to devotionals, free faith guides, florals, and more.

We hope you'll celebrate with us: two years of Ruby Doodle, 17,000 friends, and a brand-new chapter with Ivory Road. Thank you for coloring these past two years with us, and for joining us on the road ahead.
