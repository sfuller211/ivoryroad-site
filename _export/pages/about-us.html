We started Ivory Road because we love working together and we love encouraging others. That's the simplest way to say it.

Scott and Melody Fuller have been building things together for years, from ministry to education to creative projects that didn't fit neatly into any one category. Ivory Road is the place where all of it comes together. Not as a brand strategy. As an overflow of who we are and what we believe.

THE REAL TALK SERIES

Scott spent nearly a decade in paid youth ministry, first as a youth pastor's assistant, then as a junior high pastor. He sat in rooms with teenage guys who were carrying things they had no words for. He watched as someone finally gave them language for what they were feeling. And he looked for resources honest enough to meet them where they actually were.

He didn't always find them.

Real Talk is what he would have wanted then. A faith-based series for teen guys, and the parents and leaders who walk alongside them, built around one conviction: identity is revealed from the inside out through God's truth, not the mirror the world holds up.

It's not a curriculum written at a distance. It came from real ministry, real conversations, and thirty years of watching young people navigate identity, faith, and the weight of growing up.

THE REST OF WHAT WE MAKE

Ivory Road is also home to Ruby Doodle, a coloring book collection we started two years ago that has grown to a community of 17,000 friends. Because sometimes a few minutes with a coloring page can feel like a deep breath in the middle of a busy day. That matters too.

We also create free resources for teen guys and parents, practical tools designed to encourage and equip. Because we believe the right resource in the right hands at the right moment can change something.

WHAT IVORY ROAD IS

It's two people, Scott and Melody, who love working together, love their faith, and love finding ways to encourage others with what they've been given.

We're rooted in faith. We're born out of generosity, not just business. And we believe that beauty, faith, and encouragement can meet people right where they are.

We're glad you're here.
