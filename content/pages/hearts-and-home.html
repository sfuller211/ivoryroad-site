Real Talk Hearts & Home

A free guide for parents of teen boys.

If things feel quieter, heavier, or harder to reach right now, you're not alone.

Maybe he used to talk more, and now you get one-word answers.

Maybe you're seeing frustration, silence, or distance, and you're not sure how to respond.

Maybe you've wondered if you're saying too much. Or not enough.

You're not failing. You're not behind. And you're not alone.

This isn't a parenting formula or a list of rules. It's a simple, practical guide to help you connect with your son in real, everyday moments without forcing conversations or getting it perfect.

Inside the guide

  • Simple ways to open conversation without pressure
  • Real questions that actually lead somewhere
  • Small moments that build trust over time
  • Practical ways to respond to emotions without shutting them down

This guide will help you

  • Create space for real conversations without forcing them
  • Respond in ways that keep the door open
  • Build trust even when things feel tense
  • Stay connected while he's figuring things out
