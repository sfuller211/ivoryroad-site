Teen guys are dealing with real things. Identity, pressure, emotions, faith. And most of them aren’t talking about it. Not because they don’t want to. Because no one’s created a space where it feels okay.

Real Talk gives you that space. The Leader Guide was built for youth pastors and small group leaders who want to take a group through the series without having to build the framework from scratch.

It’s designed to be used with the Student Book. Students can read independently between sessions or as part of the group.

What the Leader Guide includes

  • 12 session outlines that follow the Student Book chapter by chapter, one session per chapter
  • Icebreakers and discussion questions calibrated to teen guys, not too heavy, not too light
  • Key themes and facilitator notes for each session
  • Guidance on creating the kind of safety that gets guys to actually talk
