Dr. Scott Fuller

Author of the Real Talk series.

Dr. Scott Fuller holds a PhD focused on adolescent development and faith formation. He has spent years working with teen boys, parents, and youth leaders, listening, learning, and writing about what actually helps during one of the most formative seasons of a young person's life.

His work centers on three things: identity, emotions, and real-life faith. Not theory for its own sake, but practical, grounded support for the moments that actually come up, when a teen pulls back, when he does not know who he is, when faith feels abstract and life feels heavy.

Scott is the author of the Real Talk series, which includes a student book, workbook, parent guide, and leader guide. The series grew out of his work with teen boys and a conviction that they need more than information. They need someone in their corner who takes them seriously.

He and his wife Melody live in the Dallas-Fort Worth area. They are parents of teens themselves, which means this work is personal, not just professional.
